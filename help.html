Offers and messages

Offers and messages are marketplace communication tools. They do not process payment. Keep conversations tied to the listing and never send passwords or one-time codes.

Prices and currency

The seller’s asking currency is authoritative. A local-currency amount, when shown, is an estimate for comparison and can change with exchange rates.

Condition and device checks

Read the structured condition, battery, network, repair, accessories, and delivery fields. For phones, sellers can submit an optional IMEI for private duplicate checks and moderator follow-up.

Reporting a problem

Use Report on the device or seller page when possible so moderators receive the exact listing context. For account or general support, use the Contact page.
