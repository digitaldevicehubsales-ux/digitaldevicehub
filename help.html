Buying a device

Use Marketplace filters for category, brand, model, condition, price, country, and locality. Open a listing to review seller price, condition, specifications, media, seller context, and delivery scope.

Selling and listing media

Sign in with your account, choose the exact device, add condition details, upload clear photos or one short video, set the seller asking price, location, and delivery scope, then submit for review.

Start a listing →

Offers and messages

Offers and messages are marketplace communication tools. They do not process payment. Keep conversations tied to the listing and never send passwords or one-time codes.

Review safety guidance →

Saved devices and searches

Signed-in users can save favorite devices and store marketplace searches in their account so the same filters are easy to revisit.

Prices and currency

The seller’s asking currency is authoritative. A local-currency amount, when shown, is an estimate for comparison and can change with exchange rates.

Condition and device checks

Read the structured condition, battery, network, repair, accessories, and delivery fields. For phones, sellers can submit an optional IMEI for private duplicate checks and moderator follow-up.

Reporting a problem

Use Report on the device or seller page when possible so moderators receive the exact listing context. For account or general support, use the Contact page.

Seller ratings and reviews

Buyer reviews require a completed marketplace order. Seller profiles show ratings only when completed-deal reviews exist.

See seller trust →

Condition grades

Listings can use New sealed, Open box, Like new, Good, Fair, For parts, or Refurbished. Used devices can also state whether they were previously used abroad or in the listing country.

See condition guidance →

Swaps and trade-ins

Some sellers can mark a listing as open to swaps. Agree the exact devices, cash difference, inspection, and handoff in messages before meeting.

Safe pickup

Meet in a busy staffed place, inspect the exact device, test key functions, confirm account locks are removed, and match accessories before handoff.

Pickup checklist →

Returns and disputes

DigitalDeviceHub does not currently promise universal returns, refunds, or escrow. Seller terms and applicable law may still apply. Use in-platform reports and disputes where available.

Read marketplace terms →

Price-drop alerts

Save a device to Favorites. If its published asking price drops in the same currency, DigitalDeviceHub creates an account notification.
