Browse iPhonesUsed iPhone guide

Condition matters.

Use New sealed, Open box, Like new, Good, Fair, For parts, or Refurbished. Used listings can also state whether the device was previously used abroad or in the listing country.

Check battery health.

For supported listings, compare the seller-provided battery percentage alongside condition, repair history and visible wear.

Check account locks.

Before pickup, confirm Find My and Activation Lock are removed and the device can be reset and activated normally.

IMEI screening has limits.

DigitalDeviceHub currently uses optional IMEI data for private duplicate screening. It does not yet claim a carrier or global blacklist check unless a listing explicitly shows one.

Buying guide

Inspect before you pay.

Check the screen, cameras, microphones, speakers, charging port, buttons, Face ID or Touch ID, cellular service, Wi-Fi, battery health, storage, serial details and included accessories. For in-person deals, meet in a busy staffed place and compare the exact device with the listing before handoff.
