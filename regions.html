Listing currencies

Sellers choose their asking currency. Buyers can choose a display currency for estimates when exchange-rate data is available.

Delivery

Availability is listing-specific: pickup, domestic shipping, international shipping, or a combination.

Verification

Email confirmation is the baseline account signal. Higher verification levels appear only when their full verification workflow is available and completed.

Payments

DigitalDeviceHub does not currently claim a universal protected checkout or escrow service. Any future protected payment option will state its supported regions and terms before payment.

Messaging alerts

In-platform messages and account notifications are available. External SMS or WhatsApp delivery is not claimed unless explicitly enabled for the user.
