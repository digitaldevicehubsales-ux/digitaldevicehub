Know what is checked, and what isn't.

Every check on DigitalDeviceHub is listed here, along with its limits. Use these signals before you deal, and keep inspecting the device yourself.

What we check

These run on every listing, or on every listing where the seller provides the detail.

  • Moderated listings

    A person reviews every listing before it is published.

  • Structured condition

    See the condition grade, repairs, battery, network and visible wear where sellers provide them.

  • Seller trust signals

    Profiles can show verification level, completed sales, ratings and response history.

  • Private IMEI screening

    Optional phone identifiers are hashed to detect duplicate listings. Plain IMEIs are not stored by the check.

  • On-platform messages

    Buyer and seller conversations stay tied to the device listing, for easier context and moderation.

  • Reports and review

    Listings and sellers can be reported straight into the moderation queue.

  • Price clarity

    The seller's asking currency stays visible. Local conversions are estimates.

  • Delivery clarity

    Each listing states pickup or shipping scope, so you can confirm logistics before committing.

Know what each badge means.

Seller badges build one step at a time. Only completed checks appear publicly.

  1. Email confirmedEmail code completed.
  2. Phone verifiedShown after a phone code.
  3. ID verifiedShown after identity review.
  4. Business verifiedShown after business review.

A badge reduces uncertainty; it never guarantees a transaction.

Inspect before handoff.

Check the exact device and confirm what is included before any money moves.

  1. Meet publiclyChoose a staffed, busy place.
  2. Check the deviceTest the screen, cameras and ports.
  3. Check account locksConfirm activation locks are removed.
  4. Match the listingConfirm storage, condition and accessories.

Checks reduce risk. They don't remove it.

Here is what each signal cannot tell you.

  • Email accessDoes not prove identity.
  • IMEI screeningDoes not prove ownership.
  • PaymentsNo universal escrow is offered.
  • DeliveryConfirm fees and handoff with the seller.
