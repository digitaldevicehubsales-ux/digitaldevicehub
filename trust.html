Trust & Safety

Know what is checked.

Use platform signals before dealing.

01

Moderated listings

Listings are reviewed before publishing.

02

Structured condition

See condition, repairs, battery, network and visible wear where sellers provide them.

03

Seller trust signals

Profiles can show verification level, completed sales, ratings and response history.

04

Private IMEI screening

Optional phone identifiers are hashed for duplicate detection. Plain IMEIs are not stored by the check.

05

On-platform messages

Buyer and seller conversations stay tied to the device listing for easier context and moderation.

06

Reports and review

Listings and sellers can be reported directly into the moderation queue.

07

Price clarity

The seller asking currency stays visible. Local conversions are estimates.

08

Delivery clarity

Each listing states pickup or shipping scope so buyers can confirm logistics before committing.

Know the limits

Checks reduce risk, not uncertainty.

Email access
Does not prove identity.

IMEI screening
Does not prove ownership.

Payments
No universal escrow is claimed.

Delivery
Confirm fees and handoff.
