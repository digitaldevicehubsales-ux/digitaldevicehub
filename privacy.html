Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective: October 3, 2026

This policy explains how DigitalDeviceHub processes information when you browse the marketplace, create an account, list a device, save activity, send messages or use moderation and support features.

Information we process

Depending on use, this can include email address, display name, account identifiers, sign-in provider information you approve, profile details, listings, media, favorites, saved searches, offers, messages, reviews, reports, device and security information, country and currency preferences, and basic technical logs.

Why we use information

  • Provide and secure accounts.
  • Operate listings, search, favorites, alerts, offers and messages.
  • Moderate marketplace activity and respond to reports.
  • Prevent abuse, investigate failures and improve reliability.
  • Meet legal obligations that apply to the service.

IMEI duplicate screening

Where available, an optional phone identifier can be normalized and hashed for duplicate-listing screening. The duplicate check does not by itself prove ownership or blacklist status.

Service providers

DigitalDeviceHub uses infrastructure and service providers for functions such as hosting, database, authentication, storage, DNS and transactional email. Providers process information only as needed for their services and under their own applicable terms.

Data sharing

DigitalDeviceHub does not sell personal information. Information may be shared where needed to provide a feature you choose, protect users or the service, respond to lawful requests, or comply with applicable law.

International processing

A worldwide service can involve processing across countries. Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers or grants additional privacy rights, DigitalDeviceHub will apply those requirements to the relevant processing.

Retention and security

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the service, security, moderation, disputes and legal obligations. Technical and organizational safeguards are used, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

You can update available account information, stop using the service, and request access, correction or deletion where applicable. Some records may need to be retained for security, fraud prevention, disputes or legal obligations. Rights vary by jurisdiction.

Country and currency preferences

Region and currency choices are used to tailor marketplace display. Seller asking prices remain in the seller's selected currency; converted amounts are estimates.

Contact

Privacy requests can be sent to digitaldevicehubsales@gmail.com.

Changes

This policy may change as the service evolves. Material updates will be reflected by the effective date where appropriate.
