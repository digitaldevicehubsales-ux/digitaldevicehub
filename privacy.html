Privacy Policy

Effective: September 15, 2026

DigitalDeviceHub provides an online device marketplace and account services. This policy explains the information we process when you use the website, create an account, sign in, list a device, save a listing, or communicate through the platform.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use DigitalDeviceHub, we may process your email address, display name, account identifiers, profile information supplied by a sign-in provider, listings you submit, messages you send through the marketplace, and basic technical information needed to operate and secure the service.

Google and other sign-in providers

If you choose a supported third-party sign-in option such as Google, the provider authenticates you and shares the account information you approve, such as your name, email address, and profile picture. DigitalDeviceHub uses this information only to create or sign you into your account and provide account-related features. We do not receive your Google password.

How we use information

  • To create, authenticate, and secure user accounts.
  • To operate marketplace features such as listings, favorites, and messages.
  • To prevent abuse, troubleshoot problems, and improve reliability.
  • To send account-related emails such as one-time sign-in codes.

Service providers

DigitalDeviceHub uses service providers to operate core infrastructure, including authentication, database, hosting, DNS, and transactional email services. These providers process information only as needed to provide those services under their own terms and privacy commitments.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared when necessary to provide the service, comply with law, protect users or the platform, or complete a transaction feature that you choose to use.

Data retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, maintain security, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. We use technical and organizational measures intended to protect account information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may stop using DigitalDeviceHub at any time. Where account-management features are available, you may use them to update or remove information associated with your account. You can also revoke DigitalDeviceHub's access from the security settings of a third-party sign-in provider.

Children

DigitalDeviceHub is not intended for children under the age required to consent to online services in their jurisdiction.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service evolves. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be raised through DigitalDeviceHub's published support channels on the website.
