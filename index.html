    What we check, and what a check can't prove.

    Checks reduce risk. They don't remove it. Here is exactly where the line sits, so you can decide with your eyes open.

    We check

    • Moderated listings

      A person reviews every listing before it goes live.

    • Structured condition

      Grade, repairs, battery, network and visible wear, where the seller provides them.

    • Private IMEI screening

      Phone identifiers are hashed to spot the same device listed twice. Plain IMEIs are not stored.

    • Price clarity

      The seller's currency stays visible. Local conversions are estimates.

    • Delivery clarity

      Each listing states pickup or shipping, so you know the logistics before you commit.

    A check doesn't prove

    • !
      Identity, from an email

      A confirmed email shows inbox access, not who someone is.

    • !
      Ownership, from an IMEI

      Screening catches duplicates. It can't show the device belongs to the seller.

    • !
      Payment protection

      There is no universal escrow. Pay only once you have inspected the device.

    • !
      Delivery costs

      Fees and handoff details are agreed between you and the seller.
