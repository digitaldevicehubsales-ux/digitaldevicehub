Buying guide

Buying a used iPhone.

A practical checklist for comparing condition, device history, battery, locks, price and handoff.

Understand device history

A used device may have been previously used in the same country where it is now listed or may have been imported after use elsewhere. Neither history guarantees better condition. Inspect the individual device, battery, repair history, network status and visible wear instead of relying on origin alone.

Understand condition grades

New sealed should be unopened. Open box means the packaging has been opened but the device should show little or no use. Like new should have minimal visible wear. Good allows normal wear. Fair means visible wear while remaining usable. For parts is not presented as fully working. Refurbished means the device has been restored or repaired and should disclose relevant repair information.

Check battery health

Battery percentage can materially affect daily use and future replacement cost. Compare the seller-provided battery health with the device age and condition. If the percentage shown in the listing differs from the phone during inspection, ask why before continuing.

Check activation locks

Before paying, confirm Find My is turned off, the previous owner is signed out, and the iPhone can be erased and activated without asking for another person's Apple Account credentials.

Understand IMEI screening

DigitalDeviceHub can privately hash an optional IMEI to detect duplicate identifiers across listings. This does not currently prove ownership or confirm blacklist status unless a supported third-party check is explicitly shown.

Inspect the hardware

Test the display, touch input, cameras, flash, microphones, speakers, charging port, physical buttons, vibration, Face ID or Touch ID, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and cellular connectivity. Compare storage, colour and accessories with the listing.

Meet safely

For pickup, choose a busy staffed place. Keep the listing and messages available during inspection. Do not share passwords or one-time codes. Agree any swap device, cash difference and accessories before handoff.

Compare the asking price

Use comparable listings for the same model, storage, condition and device history. A very low price is a reason to verify details more carefully, not proof of a bargain.
